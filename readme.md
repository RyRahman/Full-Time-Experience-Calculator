Full-Time Experience Calculator is a lightweight, client-side web utility designed for HR professionals, recruiters and job seekers to accurately tally cumulative full-time service across multiple positions. Built to streamline qualification checks, salary step evaluations and service verifications, the tool eliminates manual date arithmetic and flags concurrent employment timelines automatically.

Calculating total career experience across disparate job roles often introduces calculation errors, especially when accounting for concurrent or overlapping tenures. The Full-Time Experience Calculator provides an intuitive, browser-based workflow to input start and end dates (MM/YYYY), immediately compute individual and aggregate service lengths, and generate audit-ready documentation.

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Multi-Period Aggregation**: Calculate total full-time service across unlimited employment roles.
* **Automatic Overlap Detection**: Identifies overlapping date ranges and highlights rows for quick review.
* **Smart Date Input**: Accepts simple `MM/YYYY` input formatting with real-time validation.
* **Years & Months Formatting**: Automatically converts raw month counts into formatted years and months.
* **Print / PDF Export**: Features print-specific styling to generate clean, professional experience summaries.
* **Privacy-First**: Runs 100% in your browser—no data is sent to external servers.
